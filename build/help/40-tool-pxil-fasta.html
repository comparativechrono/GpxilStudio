pxil pictures → FASTA

pxil reads the sequences back out of pxil pictures and writes them as FASTA, or writes what each picture says about itself as JSON. Because a pixel’s value is a residue’s place in the picture’s alphabet, the sequence comes back exactly, whatever the colours.

The form

The pxil files
One or more pxil pictures: choose them one at a time from the list and press Add. The pictures that Genes, transcripts and proteins as pxil pictures just wrote can go straight in, from the button under its result.
Their metadata (JSON) instead of their sequences
Each picture’s metadata: what kind of sequence it is, its IDs and name, its alphabet and length, and for a gene’s pictures its contig, coordinates, exons, coding region, genetic code, source, and the sample, haplotype and notes of one made from a person.
Save as
The FASTA (or JSON) file, in ~/results.

What it writes

A FASTA record for each picture, named with its ID, name and kind, such as >ENSP00000269305 TP53-201 protein, 60 residues a line. A picture of a person’s own version of a sequence has the person and the haplotype joined to its ID – >ENSP00000269305_HG002-hap1 TP53-201 protein – so the reference’s protein and each haplotype’s keep names of their own in one file, as an alignment program needs.

In the terminal

gpxil2pxil /data/examples/HG002_chr17.gpxil.tif TP53 --annotation /data/examples/TP53.gtf --kinds protein --haplotypes all -o ~/results/pxil/TP53
pxil ~/results/pxil/TP53/*.protein.pxil.png -o ~/results/TP53_proteins.fa
pxil --info ~/results/pxil/TP53/TP53-201_ENSP00000269305_HG002-hap2.protein.pxil.png
Option
FILE …one or more pxil files
--infothe metadata, as JSON, instead of the sequences
--line Nresidues per FASTA line (60)
-o FILEwrite to a file; without it, print

On your own computer, python -m pxil does the same.

In Python

import pxil

sequence, meta = pxil.read("/home/user/results/pxil/TP53/TP53-201_ENSP00000269305_HG002-hap2.protein.pxil.png")
print(meta["name"], meta["kind"], meta["length"], meta.get("notes"))
print(sequence[60:80])

pxil.write("/home/user/results/my.transcript.pxil.png", "ATGGAGGAGCCGCAGTCAGATCC", kind="transcript", id="my-1")
print(pxil.read("/home/user/results/my.transcript.pxil.png")[0])
