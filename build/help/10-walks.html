Walkthroughs

Each walkthrough takes you through one job from beginning to end, with a button for every step. They use the examples – HG002’s chromosome 17, his variants around TP53, the GRCh38 reference and the specification’s worked example – so you can follow one through before you try it with files of your own.

You’ll also find most of these jobs on the first page of the Tools tab, under Common tasks, each with its tools in the order you need them.
