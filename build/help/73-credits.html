Ideas, standards and data

  • The genome pixel image is Guenter Albrecht-Buehler’s idea: a genome drawn as pictures, one pixel a base. gpxil takes it from one sequence to a person’s genome, with their genotype at every base, and the gpxi palette keeps his four greys.
  • gpxil files are TIFF files, and they name their reference sequences by the GA4GH’s refget checksums. Variants come from VCF files, and bases are written in the IUPAC’s codes.
  • The GRCh38 reference is the Genome Reference Consortium’s, as Ensembl release 116 gives it, with Ensembl’s genes and transcripts, and MANE’s choice of transcripts; the Gpxil browser made them into the files the studio reads.
  • HG002’s genome in the examples is the Genome in a Bottle Consortium’s benchmark (NIST), version 4.2.1, for a man of the Personal Genome Project who agreed to its release; the examples were made from it with gpxiltools. TP53’s transcripts in TP53.gtf are Ensembl’s.
  • The worked example is the gpxil specification’s.
